# Grovedock Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 19 October 2026
**Last updated:** 19 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data **Kabir Narang** (trading as "Grovedock", "we", "us"), a sole proprietorship at [REGISTERED ADDRESS], India, collects through Grovedock and our website https://grovedock.com, why, how long we keep it, who we share it with, and what rights you have.

We wrote it to meet India's **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023** ("DPDP Act") and **DPDP Rules, 2025**, and the **Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011** that still apply until the DPDP Act fully takes effect.

This notice is available in English. If you want it in any language listed in the Eighth Schedule to the Constitution of India, email grievance@grovedock.com and we will provide it.

## 1. Summary

- We collect the minimum we need to run a source-control service: who you are (name, email), how you sign in, what you and your agents do in the Service, and billing records.
- **Your code is yours.** We don't read it except to run, support or secure the Service or when the law requires, and **we never use it to train AI models**.
- **No advertising or tracking cookies.** No selling of data.
- You can access, correct, export and delete your data, and complain to our Grievance Officer: Kabir Narang, grievance@grovedock.com.

## 4. What we don't do

- We don't sell or rent personal data.
- We don't use advertising, cross-site tracking, or third-party analytics cookies.
- We don't train AI or machine-learning models on your code or personal data.
- We don't make decisions about you using fully automated profiling.

| Item | Type | Purpose | Lifetime |
|---|---|---|---|

No consent banner is required for these because they are essential, but you can clear them at any time in your browser (you will be signed out). If we ever add analytics, we will update this section and ask for consent where required.

## 7. Transfers outside India

Grovedock's servers and data are in India. Some providers above may process limited data outside India (Google sign-in and Cloudflare's global network). The DPDP Act allows transfers outside India except to countries the Government restricts by notification; we will not transfer personal data to any restricted country and will follow any conditions the Government sets.

## 8. How long we keep data

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Account and organisation data | While your account is active. Deleted within **30 days** after you delete your account, then rolls out of backups within **35 days** more. |
| Customer Content (repositories) | Until you delete it. Deleted from primary storage within **30 days** of deletion; removed from encrypted backups within **35 days** after that. |
| Security, access and audit logs (incl. IP addresses) | **180 days** within India today (CERT-In Directions 2022). From **May 2027**, DPDP Rules 6 and 8(3) require keeping logs of personal-data processing for **at least one year**; we will move to **1 year** by then. |
| Agent activity logs | **12 months**, or shorter if you delete the organisation (subject to the security-log rule above). |
| Billing and invoice records | **8 years** from the end of the relevant financial year. |
| Support emails | **2 years** after the conversation ends. |
| Inactive Free accounts | If an account has had no sign-in for **24 months**, we will email you at least **48 hours** (we intend to give 30 days) before deletion, so you can sign in to keep it. |

Where the law requires us to keep data for longer, we keep only that data, only for that purpose.

## 9. How we protect data

- Encryption in transit (TLS) for all connections to the Service; services on the same server talk only over its internal loopback interface.
- Server disks and backups are encrypted at rest.
- Access to production servers is limited to the owner, with SSH keys and multi-factor authentication, and every access is logged.
- Sign-in and access tokens are stored only as hashes; we don't store passwords.
- Rate limiting, monitoring and logging to detect misuse.
- Contracts with sub-processors requiring confidentiality and security.

No system is perfectly secure, but we will tell you if a breach affects you (section 11).

## 10. Your rights and how to use them

You can ask us to:

1. **Access** — get a summary of the personal data we hold about you, what we do with it, and who we've shared it with.
2. **Correct and update** — fix inaccurate or incomplete data (most can be changed in Tokens, in the account menu).
3. **Erase** — delete your personal data when it's no longer needed or you withdraw consent, unless the law requires us to keep it.
4. **Withdraw consent** — as easily as you gave it, by deleting your account or emailing us. This doesn't affect processing already done. If you withdraw, we may not be able to keep providing the Service.
5. **Nominate** — name one or more people to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to do so. Email us with the nominee's name and contact details.
6. **Complain (grievance redressal)** — to our Grievance Officer, and then to the **Data Protection Board of India** if you're not satisfied.

**How:** email grievance@grovedock.com from your account email (or use the organisation's Usage & plan tab) with your request. We may ask you to verify your identity. **We will acknowledge within 24 hours and respond within 7 days**, and in any case well within the legal maximum (currently 30 days under the 2011 Rules and 90 days under the DPDP Rules).

If your data is in a **customer's repository** (section 2), we will pass your request to that customer and help them respond.

## 11. Data breaches

If a personal data breach affects you, we will:

- **Tell you without delay** — by email and/or in the product — describing what happened, when, the likely impact on you, what we're doing about it, what you can do to protect yourself, and who to contact.
- **Tell the Data Protection Board of India** without delay and send a detailed report within **72 hours** of becoming aware (DPDP Rules, Rule 7 — once in force).
- **Report cyber-security incidents to CERT-In within 6 hours** of noticing them, as required by the CERT-In Directions of 28 April 2022.
- **Tell affected business customers within 72 hours** about breaches involving their repository data (see [DPA](./dpa.md)).

## 12. Children

Grovedock is for people aged **18 and over**. Under Indian law a "child" is anyone under 18. We do not knowingly create accounts for or process personal data of children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.
