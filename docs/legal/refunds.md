# Refund and Cancellation Policy

**Effective date:** 19 October 2026

This policy covers payments to **Kabir Narang** (trading as "Grovedock") for Grovedock and Grovedock Enterprise. Contact: grievance@grovedock.com, [REGISTERED ADDRESS].

## 1. Grovedock — monthly plans

- **Cancel anytime** from the organisation's Usage & plan tab or by emailing grievance@grovedock.com. Cancellation stops the next renewal.
- Your paid features stay active until the end of the month you've paid for, then your organisation moves to the Free plan (or is closed if you choose).
- **No refunds or credits for partial months**, unused seats or unused agents, except where required by law or in section 5 below.

## 2. Grovedock — annual plans

Choose ONE option before publishing:

**30-day pro-rata refund.** If you cancel within **30 days** of the start of an annual term (first purchase or renewal), we refund the unused portion of the annual fee pro-rata (by days remaining). After 30 days, cancellation stops the next renewal; the plan stays active until the end of the term and no refund is due.

- We will send a renewal reminder at least **7 days** before an annual plan renews. For monthly plans, Razorpay and your bank also notify you before each debit, as RBI rules require.
- Mid-term upgrades are charged pro-rata. Mid-term downgrades take effect at the next renewal.

## 3. Founding plans

Founding/early-adopter prices are discounted in exchange for early commitment. Founding annual plans are refundable pro-rata within 30 days of purchase, like other annual plans, and not after. The founding price is kept for as long as the plan is renewed without a break [CONFIRM].

## 4. Grovedock Enterprise (annual, prepaid)

- Standard: ₹2,40,000 per year. Founding: ₹1,20,000 per year.
- If you cancel within **30 days** of the start of the term **and** have not opened more than 2 support requests, we refund the fee pro-rata for the unused period.
- After 30 days the fee is non-refundable; the support term and licence continue until the end of the term and will not renew.
- If **we** end the agreement without cause, or fail to provide support as agreed and don't fix it within 30 days of your written notice, we refund the unused portion pro-rata.
- Foreign customers who paid via Skydo are refunded to the original payer via Skydo or bank transfer; currency conversion and bank charges may mean the amount received differs slightly.

## 5. When we always refund

- You were charged **twice** for the same thing, or charged after a cancellation that we confirmed.
- We **end your plan without cause** or permanently withdraw a paid feature that is a material part of your plan — pro-rata refund of prepaid fees.
- The Service had a prolonged outage that we caused — at our discretion, a credit or pro-rata refund (no SLA is promised).
- Anything else required by applicable law.

## 6. How to request a refund

Email grievance@grovedock.com from your account email with the subject "Refund request", your organisation name, invoice/payment ID and the reason. We acknowledge within **2 business days** and decide within **7 business days**.

## 7. Refund timelines

- Approved refunds are initiated within **5 business days** of approval, to the **original payment method** via Razorpay.
- Razorpay states that normal refunds usually reach the customer's bank account or card in **5–7 working days** (it can take up to 10 working days depending on the bank). Razorpay cannot process normal refunds for payments older than 6 months; in that case we will refund by bank transfer.
- ## 8. Chargebacks

Please contact us before raising a chargeback — most issues are fixed faster that way. If a chargeback is raised, we may suspend the account until it is resolved.

## 9. Free plan

The Free plan costs nothing, so there is nothing to refund. You can delete your account at any time.
